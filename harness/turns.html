-- what lance_vector_search really is SELECT image_id, l2_distance(clip_emb, :query_vector) AS _distance FROM merch_images ORDER BY _distance LIMIT 500;
What does this do?
claude-opus-4-5 · 12.4s · 31.2k in · 780 out

It is a brute-force k-nearest-neighbour scan: every row's clip_emb is compared against the query vector, sorted by distance, and the closest 500 come back. No index is consulted, which is why the plan reads the whole merch_images table.
